Lawn care

Lawn Care & Maintenance in Waxhaw, NC

A great lawn isn’t a weekly mow. It’s the right height, the right season and the right grass — done consistently.

Get a Lawn Care Estimate (803) 487-5240

How we approach lawn maintenance.

Most lawns around Waxhaw are one of two grasses: Fescue, which loves fall and struggles in July, or Bermuda, which loves heat and goes dormant in winter. Treat them the same and one of them loses. Every lawn we take on gets a schedule built for its turf type.

Weekly, that means mowing at the correct seasonal height, sharp string edging along every walk and curb, crack weeding, and blowing off the driveway, porch and patio before we leave. Seasonally, it means pre-emergent before the weeds come, aeration when the roots can use it, and overseeding when seed will actually take.

What’s included

01Mowing every 7–10 days at the right seasonal height
02Edging along sidewalks, driveways and curbs
03Crack weeding and blowing off hard surfaces
04Core aeration & overseeding
05Sod installation and lawn renovation
06Pre-emergent and spot weed control (licensed applicator)
When to call

Signs it’s time.

  • Thin or bare patches after summer
  • Weeds coming back every spring
  • Clay that stays hard and won’t drink water
  • You’re spending weekends on the yard

FAQ

Lawn Maintenance: questions, answered.

How often do you mow?

Every 7–10 days during the growing season, adjusted for weather and growth.

What’s the difference between weekly lawn care and the lawn program?

Weekly lawn care is mowing, edging and blowing. The lawn program bundles that with weed control, quarterly pruning, seasonal treatments for your grass type and 8 weeks of fall leaf cleanup for one flat monthly price.

Do you treat weeds?

Yes. We hold a North Carolina Pesticide Application License and apply pre-emergent and spot treatments as part of our program.
