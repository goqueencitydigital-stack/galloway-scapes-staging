Simple billing. No seasonal spikes.

The program bills a flat monthly amount, due the first week of each month, all year. That evens out the busy growing season and the quieter winter months, so you always know what the yard costs.

Cancel any time with written notice. Service continues through the end of that month, and there’s no penalty for leaving.

FAQ

Lawn program questions.

What’s included every visit?

Mowing every 7–10 days at the right seasonal height, edging along walks, drives and curbs, crack weeding, and blowing off the driveway, porch and patio.

How does billing work?

One flat monthly rate, due the first week of each month, all year — so busy months and quiet months cost the same.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Give written notice any time; service continues through the end of that month.

What’s the difference from weekly lawn care?

Weekly lawn care is the mow-edge-blow visit. The program adds weed control, seasonal treatments, quarterly pruning of ornamentals up to 10 feet, and eight weeks of fall leaf cleanup.

Do you treat Fescue and Bermuda differently?

Yes. Fescue is cool-season and is aerated and overseeded in fall. Bermuda is warm-season and is aerated in early summer. Each has its own schedule.
