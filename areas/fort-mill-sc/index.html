What Fort Mill yards need.

Close lot spacing in Fort Mill communities means water from one yard often ends up in the next. Downspouts dump onto shared side yards, and backyards that slope toward the house hold water after storms. We regrade to move water away from foundations, extend downspouts underground and add French drains where surface fixes aren’t enough.

Once the grade is right, the lawn can actually thrive. That’s when sod, aeration and a weekly schedule turn a patchy yard into the one people slow down to look at.
