Drainage and Grading Solutions for Communities

Standing water, erosion, and uneven ground are common concerns in HOA communities. In Rock Hill, heavy rainstorms can quickly overwhelm poorly graded areas, leading to muddy turf, dying grass, and potential foundation issues near shared structures.

Drainage and grading solutions are often essential components of long-term lawn maintenance. Galloway Scapes, LLC provides grading and leveling services to improve water flow and reduce pooling in common areas. Proper grading supports healthier turf by preventing root rot and soil compaction.

Common drainage solutions include:

  • Regrading low-lying lawn sections
  • Installing or improving surface drains
  • Adjusting swales to direct water flow
  • Addressing erosion near sidewalks and entrances

In some cases, drainage improvements require coordination with engineers, utility providers, or specialty contractors. As an independent company, Galloway Scapes, LLC may work with third-party providers when permitting, excavation equipment, or additional expertise is required.

Grading and drainage outcomes depend on soil type, slope, rainfall intensity, and existing infrastructure. Addressing these issues proactively helps HOAs in Rock Hill maintain safe, attractive green spaces that withstand seasonal storms.
