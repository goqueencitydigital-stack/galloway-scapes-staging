FAQ

Questions homeowners ask first.

What areas does Galloway Scapes serve?

We’re based in Waxhaw, NC and work throughout Union County, NC and York and Lancaster Counties, SC — including Weddington, Marvin, Wesley Chapel, Indian Trail, Monroe, Indian Land, Fort Mill, Tega Cay and Rock Hill.

Are estimates really free?

Yes. We walk the property, talk through what you want and send a written estimate at no cost and with no obligation.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Galloway Scapes is licensed, bonded and insured, and holds a North Carolina Pesticide Application License and an Ornamental & Turf License.

Do you work with both Fescue and Bermuda lawns?

Yes. Our lawn program runs a different seasonal schedule for cool-season Fescue and warm-season Bermuda, because they need fertilizer, aeration and weed control at different times of year.

How do I know if I need a French drain or regrading?

If water sheets across the surface toward the house, regrading or a swale usually fixes it. If the ground stays soggy for days after rain, a subsurface drain like a French drain is often the better answer. We’ll tell you which after we see where the water goes.

Do you handle commercial and HOA properties?

Yes. Along with homes, we maintain commercial properties and HOA common areas, including entrance beds, seasonal color and cleanups.
