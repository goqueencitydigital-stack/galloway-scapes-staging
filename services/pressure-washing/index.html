The complete guide

Pressure washing and soft washing in Waxhaw and the Charlotte region: the complete guide

The short answer

Pressure washing uses high water pressure to clean hard surfaces like concrete driveways and sidewalks, while soft washing uses low pressure and a cleaning solution to safely remove algae, mildew and dirt from siding and roofs. Around Waxhaw, humidity and shade make green algae on siding and black streaks on roofs very common, and choosing the right method protects the surface.

01What is the difference between pressure washing and soft washing?

Pressure washing relies on force. A high-pressure stream, often paired with a rotary surface cleaner, strips dirt, mildew and grime from durable surfaces such as poured concrete. It is fast and effective where the surface can take it.

Soft washing relies on chemistry. A cleaning solution is applied at low pressure, allowed to dwell so it can kill algae, mildew and lichen, and then rinsed gently. Because the growth is killed rather than just blasted off, results tend to last longer, and there is far less risk of damaging siding, trim, window seals, roof shingles or painted surfaces. Galloway Scapes matches the method to each surface on the property.

  • Soft wash: vinyl siding, painted surfaces, stucco, roofs, soffits and trim
  • Pressure wash: concrete driveways, sidewalks and many patios
  • Adjusted approach: wood decks and some pavers need lower pressure and care
  • Pre-treatment: oil spots and heavy organic stains often need a pre-treat

02Why do homes in this area get green siding and black roof streaks?

The Carolina climate is warm and humid for much of the year, and many neighborhoods have mature trees that keep north- and shade-facing walls damp. That combination is ideal for algae and mildew. The green film on vinyl siding and the black streaks running down asphalt shingles are usually living organisms, most often algae, not just dirt.

Left alone, roof algae holds moisture and can shorten the look and life of shingles. Many shingle manufacturers and roofing industry groups advise against high-pressure washing of roofs because it can strip protective granules, and recommend low-pressure cleaning instead. That is exactly what soft washing is.

From Terry

If the black streaks on your roof are mostly on the north side or under trees, they are almost certainly algae, and they come off without a pressure washer.

03How do you protect plants and landscaping during washing?

This is where a landscape company has an advantage. Before soft washing, plants and beds around the foundation are pre-wet with clean water so they absorb less runoff, sensitive plants can be covered, and surfaces are rinsed thoroughly afterward. Downspouts and runoff paths are considered so solution doesn't concentrate in one bed.

We also look at the property as a whole: outdoor furniture moved and rinsed, windows and doors closed, outlets and light fixtures protected. The goal is a clean house with the landscape looking exactly as it did before, only easier to see.

04How often should a house, roof and driveway be cleaned?

Most homes around Waxhaw benefit from a house wash about once a year, sometimes more often on heavily shaded walls. Roofs generally need soft washing less often, because a proper treatment slows regrowth for longer. Driveways and sidewalks are often cleaned yearly, or before a special event, a home listing or the holidays.

Washing pairs naturally with seasonal yard work. Many homeowners book a spring wash alongside a spring cleanup and mulch refresh, so the house, hardscape and beds all look their best at the same time.

05Can pressure washing damage concrete, wood or pavers?

Yes, when the pressure or technique is wrong. Too much pressure up close can etch concrete and leave visible wand marks, raise and splinter the grain on wood decks, wash out the joint sand between pavers, and force water behind siding. That is why technique matters as much as equipment.

On concrete we use a surface cleaner for even, streak-free results, followed by an edge rinse. On wood, pressure is kept low with a wider spray to clean without furring the grain. On pavers, we clean carefully and let you know if joints need re-sanding afterward.

FAQ

Pressure Washing: questions, answered.

How much does pressure washing a house cost in Waxhaw, NC?

The cost of house washing depends on the size and height of the home, the siding material, how much algae and mildew has built up, how accessible each side is, and whether you add the roof, driveway, sidewalks, patio or deck. A single-story ranch with light buildup is a very different job from a tall two-story home with heavy growth on shaded walls. Galloway Scapes provides free written estimates that list exactly which surfaces are included and which method will be used on each, soft wash or pressure wash. Bundling several surfaces into one visit is usually the most efficient way to get the whole property clean, and the estimate makes it easy to choose what to include.

Is soft washing safe for vinyl siding and roofs?

Yes. Soft washing was developed specifically for surfaces that high pressure can damage. Instead of force, it uses a cleaning solution applied at low pressure to kill algae, mildew and lichen, followed by a gentle rinse. On vinyl siding, that avoids forcing water behind panels or cracking older siding. On asphalt shingle roofs, it removes the black algae streaks without stripping the protective granules, which is why many shingle manufacturers recommend low-pressure cleaning over pressure washing. Because the growth is killed at the root rather than just blasted off the surface, soft-washed siding and roofs typically stay clean longer than pressure-washed ones.

Will the cleaning solution hurt my plants or lawn?

Plant protection is part of every Galloway Scapes soft wash. Before cleaning, plants and beds near the house are pre-wet with clean water so they absorb less runoff, sensitive plants can be covered, and after cleaning everything nearby is rinsed thoroughly to dilute any overspray. Runoff paths from downspouts and slopes are considered so solution doesn't collect in one bed. Because we are a landscaping company first, we pay close attention to the shrubs, perennials and turf around the foundation. If you have particularly delicate plants, a vegetable garden or a koi pond near the house, point them out on the walkthrough and we will plan extra protection for those areas.

How often should I have my driveway and sidewalks cleaned?

For most homes around Waxhaw, Indian Trail, Fort Mill and Rock Hill, cleaning concrete driveways and sidewalks once a year keeps them looking bright and prevents mildew and algae from building up in shaded areas. Driveways under trees, on north-facing lots or near irrigation overspray may need cleaning more often. Many homeowners schedule driveway cleaning in spring along with a house wash, or before a graduation party, holiday gathering or listing photos. Oil stains and rust marks may need pre-treatment and sometimes won't come out completely, especially if they are old. We will tell you on the estimate what to expect so the results match your expectations.

Do you clean decks and patios too?

Yes. Wood decks, concrete patios and stone or paver patios are all part of the service, but each surface gets a different approach. Wood is cleaned at low pressure with a wide fan so the grain isn't raised or splintered, and we can let you know whether it would benefit from sealing or staining afterward. Concrete patios are cleaned with a surface cleaner for even results. Pavers are cleaned carefully so joint sand isn't blasted out, and we will mention if the joints need re-sanding. Outdoor furniture can be moved and rinsed as part of the job. Deck and patio cleaning pairs well with a spring cleanup so the whole backyard is ready for the season.
