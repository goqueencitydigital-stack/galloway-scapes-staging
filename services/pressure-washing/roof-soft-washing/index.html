Roof Soft Washing

Those black streaks are algae. They come off without a pressure washer.

Get a Washing Estimate (803) 487-5240

How we approach roof soft washing.

Pressure on a shingle roof strips granules. We apply a soft-wash solution that breaks down algae and lichen and let it work, so stains fade without damage.

What’s included

01Algae and streak removal
02Low-pressure application
03Gutter-line rinse
When to call

Signs it’s time.

  • Black streaks on shingles
  • Moss or lichen
