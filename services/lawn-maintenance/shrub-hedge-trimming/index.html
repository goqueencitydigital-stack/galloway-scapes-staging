How we approach shrub & hedge trimming.

Shearing everything into boxes isn’t always right. Boxwoods and hollies take a crisp shape; flowering shrubs need selective pruning at the right time so you don’t cut off next year’s blooms. On our program, hedges and shrubs (and ornamental trees up to 10 ft) are trimmed four times a year.

What’s included

01Hedge shaping
02Selective pruning of shrubs and perennials
03Ornamental trees up to 10 ft
04Debris haul-off
When to call

Signs it’s time.

  • Shrubs covering windows or walks
  • Uneven, shaggy hedges
  • Dead wood inside shrubs
