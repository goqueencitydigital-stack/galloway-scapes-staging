Every good lawn, patio and drainage system starts with the shape of the ground.

Get a Grading Estimate (803) 487-5240

How we approach grading.

Grading is where most yard problems start — and where they can be fixed for good. We use our own compact track loader and mini excavator to cut high spots, fill lows, set positive slope away from the foundation and leave a smooth, compacted surface ready for sod, seed or hardscape.

Because we also install the lawn and landscape afterward, the grade is built for what goes on top of it.
