Our approach

How we approach garden & planting design.

We pick plants for the exact spot: full sun or shade, wet or dry, deer pressure, mature size. Then we arrange them in layers so something looks good every month — evergreen structure, blooming shrubs, perennials and a little seasonal color near the door.

Planting details

The small choices that make it look finished.

What we plant, where, and why, from Galloway Scapes projects around Waxhaw.

Boxwoods in fresh mulch

Evergreen boxwoods frame the porch and stay tidy all year with two trims.

Boulder accents

Pine straw tree rings

Rings around mature trees protect roots from mowers and tie the yard together.

Beds along the walk

Layered plantings soften the steps and lead guests to the front door.

How it works

Four steps. No surprises.

From the first call to the final cleanup, you deal with the same owner-run crew.

01

Call or send the form

Tell us what’s bothering you about the yard. We get back to you within 24 hours.

02
