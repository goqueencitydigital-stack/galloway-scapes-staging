Landscape Design

Outdoor Living Spaces

A backyard should be a place you go, not just something you look at.

Get a Design Estimate (803) 487-5240

How we approach outdoor living design.

The outdoor spaces people use most are simple: a level place to sit, a fire, a path to get there, and plantings that give a little privacy. We grade the area, set edging, lay gravel or stone, build or set the fire pit and finish the surrounding beds.

What’s included

01Fire pit areas
02Gravel and stone patios
03Garden rooms and raised planters
04Paths and stepping stones

Planting details

The small choices that make it look finished.

What we plant, where, and why, from Galloway Scapes projects around Waxhaw.

Boxwoods in fresh mulch

Evergreen boxwoods frame the porch and stay tidy all year with two trims.

Boulder accents

A single boulder anchors the bed and gives low plantings something to grow around.

Pine straw tree rings

Rings around mature trees protect roots from mowers and tie the yard together.

Beds along the walk

Layered plantings soften the steps and lead guests to the front door.

How it works

Four steps. No surprises.

01

Call or send the form

Tell us what’s bothering you about the yard. We get back to you within 24 hours.

02

Walk the property

We look at slope, soil, drainage paths, turf type and how you actually use the space. No guessing from a photo.

03

A written estimate

You get a plain-language scope and price. No surprise add-ons after the crew shows up.

04

Owner-led work and cleanup

Terry is on the job. Beds edged, debris hauled off and the driveway blown clean before we leave.
