Landscaping for Weddington’s Larger Lots

Weddington is known for big, wooded lots and long frontages. That changes how a property should be designed and maintained.

Get a Free Estimate in Weddington (803) 487-5240

What Weddington yards need.

With larger lots, the front yard is often a long sweep of lawn with trees and beds spread far apart. Good design here is about scale: wider bed lines, groupings of shrubs that read from the road, and tree rings that tie mature hardwoods into the landscape instead of leaving them as islands.

Maintenance is a bigger job, too. We bring commercial stand-on mowers for wide-open turf, and our fall program covers eight consecutive weeks of leaf removal — which matters when a property has dozens of oaks.

Because many Weddington homes sit on gentle slopes with long driveways, drainage problems show up as washouts along the drive, soggy low corners and erosion where gutters dump. Those get a drainage plan, not just more mulch.

Design at the right scale

Bed lines and plantings sized for an acre, not a quarter-acre.

Leaf season handled

Eight straight weeks of fall leaf removal on program properties.

Driveway washouts

Swales, downspout lines and French drains for long, sloped drives.
