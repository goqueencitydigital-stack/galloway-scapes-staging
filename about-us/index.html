Our story

Quoted by the owner. Done right.

Terry Galloway started Galloway Scapes to do landscaping the way he’d want it done at his own house: show up when we say, explain the work in plain language, and leave the property cleaner than we found it.

The company is licensed, bonded and insured, and holds a North Carolina Pesticide Application License and an Ornamental & Turf License. Those licenses are why we can handle weed control and turf treatments in-house instead of subcontracting them.

Today the crew handles everything from weekly mowing routes to French drains, sod installs, gravel patios and landscape lighting, across Union County, NC and York and Lancaster Counties, SC. Residential, commercial and HOA properties.
